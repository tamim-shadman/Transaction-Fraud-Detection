## 1. Executive Summary

Technical storytelling in high-stakes domain analytics—such as financial fraud detection, regulatory compliance, and machine learning model validation—cannot rely on marketing rhetoric, ungrounded superlatives, or disconnected slides. Every narrative statement, visual representation, and executive summary must be anchored to verifiable empirical evidence.

This methodology defines how the Transaction Fraud Detection platform structures narratives across **Exploration** and **Presentation** modes, how the **7-Stage Project Walkthrough** guides reviewers through research progression, and how **Evidence Governance** prevents narrative fabrication.

## 2. The 7-Stage Project Storytelling Framework

Every research initiative (`PROJ-01` through `PROJ-07`) represents a distinct investigation within the broader Sparkov card-fraud research program. To provide consistent, logical progression without sacrificing technical depth, all project walkthroughs follow a standardized 7-stage framework:

```mermaid
graph LR
    S1[1. The Problem] --> S2[2. The Approach]
    S2 --> S3[3. The Implementation]
    S3 --> S4[4. The Evidence]
    S4 --> S5[5. The Interpretation]
    S5 --> S6[6. The Generated Results]
    S6 --> S7[7. Technical Lessons]
```

### Stage 1: The Problem (Context & Objective)
- **Objective**: Establish why the investigation was initiated and what analytical challenge it addresses.
- **Required Content**: Baseline transaction volume, prevalence rate (0.521% / 0.58%), class imbalance ratio (1:171), and business cost of fraudulent card activity.
- **Evidence Anchor**: Initial schema contracts and class distribution tables (`phase0_contracts`, `phase0_missing_summary`).

### Stage 2: The Approach (Methodology & Design)
- **Objective**: Explain the theoretical and methodological strategy adopted by the research team.
- **Required Content**: Modeling paradigm selection (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost), spatio-temporal embedding strategies, or non-parametric statistical hypothesis testing.
- **Evidence Anchor**: Phase definitions and research questions (`manifest.phases`, `rqs`).

### Stage 3: The Implementation (Engineering & Pipeline)
- **Objective**: Document the actual code and data engineering executed, rather than an idealized theoretical architecture.
- **Required Content**: 72 engineered features, rolling velocity aggregates (1h, 24h, 7d, 30d), target encoding, walk-forward temporal cross-validation folds, and notebook cell execution ranges.
- **Evidence Anchor**: Feature metadata tables (`phase6_features`), execution logs (`phase8_logs`), and cell references.

### Stage 4: The Evidence (Empirical Measurements)
- **Objective**: Present the primary numerical and visual evaluation results.
- **Required Content**: Area Under the Precision-Recall Curve (PR-AUC), ROC-AUC, Catch@k cumulative recall, Mann-Whitney U test statistics, and Cramér's V effect sizes.
- **Evidence Anchor**: Evaluation curve coordinates (`phase6_baseline_curves.json`), ablation tables (`phase6_ablation.csv`), and statistical effect tables (`phase5_effects.csv`).

### Stage 5: The Interpretation (What the Data Proves & Uncertainty)
- **Objective**: Explain what the evidence substantiates, while explicitly acknowledging boundaries and uncertainties.
- **Required Content**: Separation of in-domain walk-forward performance (PR-AUC 0.9510) from out-of-distribution test transfer (PR-AUC 0.5575), revealing a **0.4785 OOD transfer loss**. Clarification that PR-AUC is prevalence-dependent.
- **Evidence Anchor**: Walk-forward comparison table (`phase7_wf_vs_test.csv`) and Population Stability Index drift tests (`phase6_temporal_drift.csv`).

### Stage 6: The Generated Results (Inspectable Artifacts)
- **Objective**: Provide direct access to the actual figures, plots, and tables generated by the code.
- **Required Content**: High-resolution PNG figures, interactive HTML plots, confusion matrices, and calibration curves.
- **Evidence Anchor**: Artifact registry (`manifest.figures`, `manifest.tables`) with source cell provenance.

### Stage 7: Technical Lessons (Strengths, Failure Modes & Recommendations)
- **Objective**: Synthesize operational recommendations, documented simulator artifacts, and future engineering priorities.
- **Required Content**: Demonstrated strengths (amount separation, velocity lift), observed synthetic simulator flaws (merchant coordinate jitter, 2.05% impossible velocity $>1,000\text{ km/h}$), and operational review threshold rules ($5/FP economics).
- **Evidence Anchor**: Graded findings ledger (`phase2_findings.json`) and audit notes.

## 3. Evidence Governance & Classification Tiers

To prevent unsupported claims and maintain total transparency, every metric and finding displayed in walkthroughs, presentations, and reports is assigned an **Evidence Classification Tier**:

| Classification | Badge Color | Definition & Criteria | Example in Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Directly Verified** | Green | Empirically derived from inspectable output files, raw test records, or notebook execution logs. | Total Ingested Transactions = **1,852,394** (`phase0_contracts.csv`) |
| **Recomputed** | Blue | Independently recalculated by automated validation suites from primary evaluation coordinates. | Catch@1% Review Capacity = **84.32%** (`phase7_capture_at_k.csv`) |
| **Reported** | Amber | Transcribed directly from source research logs or published model notes without independent reproduction. | Baseline training duration = **14.2 seconds** (`logs/run_provenance.json`) |
| **Inferred** | Purple | Statistically derived or synthesized from multi-feature relationships or regression models. | High-risk category/night combination lift = **42.37×** (`phase2_combos.csv`) |
| **Unavailable** | Gray | Explicitly missing due to environment limitations or unperformed tests. Never replaced with zero! | Multi-seed confidence interval for single-seed runs: rendered as `—`. |

## 4. Narrative Integrity Constraints

1. **No Invented Significance**: P-values, confidence intervals, or "winner" labels are never synthesized from single-seed experiment runs.
2. **Prevalence-Sensitivity Safeguard**: Cross-family comparisons are blocked from claiming causal superiority when test class balances differ.
3. **Strict Separation of Simulation vs. Native Power BI**: The browser-based dashboard is documented as a high-fidelity simulation; native Power BI deliverables are clearly demarcated as the import-ready `powerbi/` pack.
4. **Dynamic Data Binding**: In the Report Composer, all narrative blocks bind directly to the canonical metric store (`manifest.json`, `analysis.json`). If the upstream data changes, the report content updates automatically.
